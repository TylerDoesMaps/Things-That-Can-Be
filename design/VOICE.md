# Voice

Things That Never Were is the model for how this game feels and reads. Little Big Man is the model for how its county map works.

## Writing

- Second person, inside the head of the politician being played. The narrator has that person's grudges.
- Bitter, funny and specific. Insider language, real names, real rooms. Mild profanity where the character would use it.
- A scene is a short paragraph that ends on a decision.
- Answers are written as the character talking, not as menu options. Each answer sounds like a different instinct of the same person.
- Answers rarely announce a policy position. The position is implied by who gets used, flattered or threatened.
- After a choice, a short aftermath in the voice of the world, with an image. Consequences are funny or ominous, never summarized as stat changes.
- Names and terms of art are marked in the text and can be hovered for context.
- A figure's verbal tics can carry into the answer text.

## Screens

- Portrait cards for President and Vice President around a question card with a count.
- A map screen with national support and a state summary.
- State summaries pair hard numbers with flavor attributes written as short phrases.

## Interface

No Subtext (Principle 2) covers controls, labels and data views. Narrative prose is the game itself and belongs on the scene and aftermath screens only.

## County map

- Views for margin, vote share, solid/lean and shift from the previous election.
- Hover box with place, total votes, each candidate's share and votes, margin, and held or flipped.
